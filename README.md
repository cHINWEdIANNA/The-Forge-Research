#  THE FORGE

### A Systematic Trading Research Chronicle

The Forge is an ongoing quantitative trading research project focused on developing, testing, rejecting, and validating systematic trading strategies.

The objective is not to find a strategy that looks profitable in a backtest. The objective is to identify trading systems that can survive chronological validation, realistic execution assumptions, adverse market periods, and genuinely unseen forward data.

---

## Research Philosophy

The Forge follows several core principles:

- Strategies are tested before deployment.
- Out-of-sample evidence is prioritized over attractive full-sample results.
- Strategies are frozen once forward testing begins.
- Losing research branches are documented rather than hidden.
- Drawdown, expectancy, profit factor, robustness, and execution quality matter more than win rate alone.
- Different strategy families are researched to reduce dependence on a single market hypothesis.
